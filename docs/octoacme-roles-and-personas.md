# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers design and validate user experience and interface flows. They ensure that product features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs through research, prototyping, and testing.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes
- Collaborate with Product Managers on user requirements
- Partner with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation fidelity
- Participate in design reviews and provide feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centered designs
- Validate designs through user research and testing
- Ensure design consistency across features and products
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- Collaboration with Product Managers on feature specs
- Handoff sessions with Developers
- User research findings and recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on defining user needs and acceptance criteria
- **Developers**: Work closely on implementation details and design feasibility
- **QA**: Partner on usability testing and acceptance validation
- **Project Managers**: Provide timeline estimates for design work

---

## Data Analysts

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define, collect, and analyze project and product data. They provide insights that inform prioritization, measure success, and drive continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and data collection strategies
- Build dashboards and reports for key performance indicators
- Analyze usage patterns, feature adoption, and customer behavior
- Provide data-driven insights for product decisions
- Support retrospectives with objective performance data
- Identify trends and anomalies in product metrics

### Goals
- Enable data-informed decision making
- Measure product impact and success accurately
- Surface actionable insights from data
- Improve transparency through clear reporting

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metric reviews with Product and Project Managers
- Ad-hoc analysis requests from stakeholders
- Dashboard demos and metric definitions
- Data findings in retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define success metrics and provide insight for roadmap decisions
- **Developers**: Collaborate on instrumentation and data collection
- **Project Managers**: Provide progress metrics and burndown data
- **Stakeholders**: Present findings and business impact

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the release pipeline and deployment plans across environments. They ensure that releases are smooth, predictable, and follow established processes.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows
- Manage release checklists and gate requirements
- Oversee deployment to staging and production environments
- Coordinate rollback procedures when needed
- Communicate release status to stakeholders
- Maintain release documentation and runbooks

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, low-risk releases
- Minimize deployment-related incidents
- Ensure clear communication during releases
- Continuously improve release processes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings
- Deployment status updates
- Go/no-go decisions with stakeholders
- Post-release retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinate code freeze, release branches, and deployment readiness
- **QA**: Ensure all testing gates are passed before release
- **Project Managers**: Align release timing with project milestones
- **Product Managers**: Communicate release contents and impacts

---

## Customer Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Support Liaisons act as the bridge between customers and the delivery team. They surface user pain points, provide real-world feedback, and ensure support considerations are included in planning and releases.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer feedback and pain points
- Represent customer perspective in planning discussions
- Document support requirements for new features
- Provide input on known issues and workarounds
- Coordinate support readiness for releases
- Escalate customer-impacting issues to the delivery team

### Goals
- Ensure customer voice is heard in product development
- Reduce support burden through proactive planning
- Improve customer satisfaction and product usability
- Bridge the gap between users and the development team

### Typical Communication
- Weekly feedback sessions with Product and Project teams
- Release readiness reviews
- Customer escalations and issue reports
- Support documentation and knowledge base updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Share customer insights and feature requests
- **Developers**: Communicate bugs and user-reported issues
- **Release Managers**: Coordinate support documentation and training for releases
- **Project Managers**: Provide customer perspective in planning
