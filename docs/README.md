# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub! This README serves as the **central entry point** for discovering and navigating all OctoAcme process documentation. Whether you're a new team member getting up to speed or an experienced contributor looking for specific guidance, you'll find everything you need here.

Our documentation is designed to help teams deliver high-quality products efficiently through clear processes, well-defined roles, and consistent communication practices.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a structured yet flexible approach to project delivery, centered around these core elements:

### Approach

OctoAcme's project management philosophy emphasizes:

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery**: Ship small, testable increments to enable rapid feedback and learning
- **Clear ownership**: Every project has designated Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead with well-defined responsibilities
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence, not assumptions
- **Psychological safety**: Foster an environment that encourages feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

### Project Lifecycle

Projects follow a five-stage lifecycle to ensure consistent, quality delivery:

1. **Initiation**: Define the problem statement, identify stakeholders, and establish high-level timelines
2. **Planning**: Break down work into shippable increments, identify dependencies and risks, and create detailed delivery plans
3. **Execution**: Build, test, review, and iterate on features with daily standups and weekly syncs
4. **Release & Deployment**: Deploy to production following standardized procedures with proper verification and rollback plans
5. **Retrospective**: Capture learnings, celebrate successes, and convert insights into actionable improvements

### Roles & Responsibilities

Our cross-functional teams include these key roles:

- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines what should be built, owns the product vision, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success outcomes
- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery activities, manages schedules, tracks risks and dependencies, and facilitates team communications
- **Developers**: Design, build, test, and deliver software components with a focus on maintainability and reliability
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality standards, verify acceptance criteria are met, and ensure comprehensive test coverage
- **Stakeholders**: Provide business context, requirements input, and necessary approvals at key decision points

### Communication Cadence

Regular, structured communication keeps everyone aligned:

- **Daily standups**: 15-minute team check-ins focused on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly syncs**: PM + PdM alignment meetings to review progress, risks, and upcoming work
- **Twice-weekly team standups**: Delivery team coordination (or as agreed upon by the team)
- **Monthly stakeholder updates**: Progress reports and roadmap updates for broader audiences
- **Sprint/milestone demos**: End-of-iteration showcases of completed work
- **Project boards**: GitHub Projects or similar tools for real-time workflow tracking and transparency

### Key Artifacts

OctoAcme teams maintain these essential documents:

- Project Charter / One-pager
- Roadmap and Release Plan
- Sprint/Iteration Backlog
- Acceptance Criteria & Definition of Done
- Risk Register
- Retrospective notes and action items

## How to Use These Docs

### For New Team Members
1. Start with the **Project Management Overview** to understand our philosophy and approach
2. Review **Roles and Personas** to understand team structure and responsibilities
3. Read through the phase-specific guides relevant to your current project stage

### For Project Managers
- Use the **Project Initiation Guide** when starting new projects
- Reference **Planning**, **Execution & Tracking**, and **Risk Management** guides during active delivery
- Follow the **Release & Deployment Guide** for production deployments
- Facilitate retrospectives using the **Continuous Improvement** guide

### For Developers and Contributors
- Check **Execution & Tracking** for daily workflow guidance and PR conventions
- Review **Roles and Personas** to understand how you interact with PMs and PdMs
- Consult **Release & Deployment** before production releases

### For Product Managers
- Use **Project Initiation** to validate new ideas and define success metrics
- Reference **Project Planning** when building roadmaps and prioritizing backlogs
- Review **Risk Management & Communication** for stakeholder alignment strategies

### Best Practices
- **Keep documentation updated**: Store project-specific docs in your repository's `docs/` or `.copilot/` folder
- **Use as reference**: These guides provide frameworks, not rigid rules—adapt to your project's needs
- **Leverage Copilot Spaces**: Add process docs to `.copilot/` to give GitHub Copilot context about your team's practices
- **Continuous improvement**: Suggest updates to these guides based on your team's learnings
